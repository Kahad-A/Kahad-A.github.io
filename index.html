About

Data Scientist and researcher specializing in environmental governance, political ecology, and urban development. My work focuses on how institutions, actors, and environmental systems interact to shape sustainable development outcomes.

I use data-driven methods, spatial analysis (GIS), and computational modeling to understand complex socio-environmental systems.
